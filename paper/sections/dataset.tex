\section{Dataset and Preprocessing}
\subsection{Mixture Composition}
Training uses a 13-source mixture (20\,400 sampled rows) plus 9\,600
consensus-soft rows: Open-Jev release-v2 (3\,600) and mailroom-control-v1
(2\,400), LeWiDi conv\_abuse and csc sarcasm (1\,200 each, annotator-share soft
labels), and GoEmotions (1\,200 emotion-fraction rows). Intent taxonomies
(BANKING77, CLINC150) are upweighted $3\times$; twelve public intent and
reasoning sources fill the remainder. All rows are rendered into canonical
decision records (state, typed questions with option criteria, gold labels).

\subsection{Schema Augmentation}
Augmentation (treatment E) applies option subsampling and shuffling (wide
taxonomies capped at 24 options), instruction paraphrasing, and derived binary
(noul) questions. Treatment A disables all augmentation (canonical schemas).
With augmentation the same row budget yields more decisions (39\,560 vs
31\,754), a residual we disclose rather than hide.

\subsection{Decontamination and Splits}
All rows are decontaminated against canonical test splits with 13-gram shingle
matching before training. Items are split train/calibration/test with fixed
seeds; temperature scaling is fit on calibration only, verdicts on held-out
tests. For leave-one-taxonomy-out (LOTO) runs, every row, label definition, and
augmentation variant of the held-out taxonomy is removed from training.
