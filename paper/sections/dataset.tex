\section{Dataset and Preprocessing}
\subsection{Mixture Composition}
Training uses a 13-source standard mixture (12 public sources plus
typed-decisions; 20\,400 sampled rows, intent taxonomies sampled at
$3\times$ weight) plus 9\,600 consensus-soft rows: Open-Jev release-v2 (3\,600) and mailroom-control-v1
(2\,400), LeWiDi conv\_abuse and csc sarcasm (1\,200 each, annotator-share soft
labels), and GoEmotions (1\,200 emotion-fraction rows). Intent taxonomies
(BANKING77, CLINC150) are upweighted $3\times$; twelve public intent and
reasoning sources fill the remainder. Custom rows are mapped explicitly:
Open-Jev \texttt{options} lists (\texttt{id: description}) become criteria with
annotator-share probabilities; LeWiDi soft labels map onto
\texttt{true}/\texttt{false} (abusive/non-abusive) and six sarcasm levels;
GoEmotions multi-rater rows become per-emotion fractions. All rows are rendered
into canonical decision records. The 13 standard sources are
\texttt{ag\_news}, \texttt{arc} (CC-BY-SA-4.0), \texttt{banking77} (CC-BY-4.0,
3$\times$ weight), \texttt{boolq} (CC-BY-SA-3.0), \texttt{clinc150} (CC-BY-3.0,
3$\times$ weight), \texttt{commonsense\_qa} (MIT), \texttt{mmlu} (MIT),
\texttt{mnli} (mixed, see card), \texttt{prompt\_injections},
\texttt{snli} (CC-BY-SA-4.0), \texttt{sst5}, \texttt{wanli}, and
\texttt{typed-decisions} (Apache-2.0); weights sum to 17 shares over 20\,400
rows (3\,600 each for banking77/clinc150, 1\,200 each otherwise). Customs add
Open-Jev (2 subsets, 6\,000), LeWiDi (2 configs, 2\,400), and GoEmotions
(1\,200). Licenses verified against Hub cards 2026-10-14 except
\texttt{sst5}, \texttt{wanli}, LeWiDi, Open-Jev, and GoEmotions, which remain
unaudited (see Section~6 limits).

\subsection{Schema Augmentation}
Augmentation (treatment E) applies option subsampling and shuffling (wide
taxonomies capped at 24 options), instruction paraphrasing, and derived binary
(noul) questions. Treatment A disables all augmentation (canonical schemas).
Counts below distinguish \textit{rows} (sampled mixture records) from
\textit{decisions} (rendered questions after augmentation): at the same
28\,000-row budget, E yields 40\,306 decisions vs 32\,500 for A (746 skipped
per run, logged in \texttt{run\_meta.json}), a residual we disclose rather
than hide.

\subsection{Decontamination and Splits}
All rows are decontaminated against canonical test splits with 13-gram shingle
matching before training. Decontamination uses Unsloth's 13-gram shingle
filter against the canonical test splits (typed-decisions full test plus
500-item subsamples, seed 3407, of BANKING77 and CLINC150). We disclose two
limitations. First, 75.3\% of BANKING77 and 89.3\% of CLINC150 test
utterances are shorter than 13 words, where the filter degrades to
whole-string exact matching (verified against the library source):
paraphrases and near-duplicates pass through. A post-hoc TF-IDF audit
(cosine $\geq$ 0.9, CPU) finds 502/3,080 (16.3\%) BANKING77 and 440/5,500
(8.0\%) CLINC150 test utterances with a near-duplicate in the corresponding
train pool, and cross-taxonomy leakage is negligible ($\leq$18 items per
direction). Second, only 500 of 3,080/5,500 test items enter the filter,
leaving $\sim$84--91\% of test shingles uncovered. LOTO runs remove the
held-out taxonomy's source entirely (train and test), so the residual leakage
($\sim$0.8\% of intent-train rows) cannot explain the transfer numbers; we
did not retrain and instead report this audit as a bound. Items are split train/calibration/test with fixed
seeds; temperature scaling is fit on calibration only, verdicts on held-out
tests. For leave-one-taxonomy-out (LOTO) runs, every row, label definition, and
augmentation variant of the held-out taxonomy is removed from training. Render skips are counted, not silent:
rows whose schema alone overflows and non-binary noul criteria are skipped
with logged reasons, and the per-run counts live in \texttt{run\_meta.json}
(the seed3407 run built 43\,171 decisions).
