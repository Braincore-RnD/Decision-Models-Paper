\begin{abstract}
Generative language models are increasingly misused as structured decision
routers, paying $O(T)$ sequential decoding cost and returning uncalibrated
confidences. System-One decision models instead map a state and a typed schema
to probabilities in a single forward pass. We adapt
LiquidAI LFM2.5-350M, a hybrid convolution-attention model, into a decision
 engine with a cross-attention JointSchemaHead trained via QLoRA. Across ten
 controlled training runs and eight evaluation dumps we find: (1) leave-one-
 taxonomy-out transfer is substantial but partial---43.4\% [39--48] on BANKING77 and
 55.6\% on CLINC150 against untrained-head baselines of 2.4\%/17.2\% (uniform
 chance 1.3\%/0.67\%), versus 81.3\%/72.9\% in-distribution
 means over three seeds---but at or below a zero-shot embedding baseline
 (68.0\%/56.9\% full-test with no training); with canonical (unaugmented)
 training the held-out numbers are 35.4\% [31--39] and 54.8\% [50--59], so
 augmentation buys $+$8.6\,pt on BANKING77 (CIs nearly disjoint) and nothing on
 CLINC150; (2) an earlier 36\%\,$\to$\,83\% augmentation jump was a
 context-truncation confound, not semantic robustness: row-matched ablation is
 a null result (typed-ext 0.563$\pm$0.033 vs 0.549$\pm$0.011, a 1.4\,pt gap
 inside 7.6\,pt seed spread), so in-distribution canonical and augmented
 training are indistinguishable from seed noise; augmentation's verified role
 is transfer plus memory savings; (3) calibration does not
 transfer---ECE rises from 0.026 in-distribution to 0.366 under shift, and
 in-distribution temperatures recover only part of it (0.220 vs 0.104 oracle)---yet
 error-detection (AUROC 0.87\,$\to$\,0.73) survives, and realistic autonomous
 coverage collapses from 63\% to near zero. On the official full tests recipe E
 gives BANKING77 75.6\% [74.1--77.0] (3\,080) and CLINC150 67.6\% [66.3--68.9]
 (5\,500), $\sim$5\,pt below the 500-subsets; a 1\,000-item calibration slice
 with temperature $T{=}1.72$ cuts ECE 0.119\,$\to$\,0.039 on BANKING77 and
 transfers to CLINC150 (0.091\,$\to$\,0.048); 1\,000 OOS utterances abstain
 33.5\% [30.6--36.5] of the time at $\tau{=}0.5$ with mean max-probability
 0.637, i.e.\ overconfident out of scope. All checkpoints, dumps, and logs are
 archived for reproduction.
\end{abstract}
