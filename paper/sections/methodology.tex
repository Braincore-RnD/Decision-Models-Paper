\section{Proposed Methodology}
\subsection{Backbone and Decision Head}
We adapt \texttt{LiquidAI/LFM2.5-350M}~\cite{liquid-lfm2} (16 layers: 10
double-gated convolutional LIV blocks plus 6 grouped-query attention blocks)
with QLoRA (rank 16) following the Unsloth decision recipe~\cite{unsloth-decision}.
A JointSchemaHead projects final hidden states to width 256, routes candidate
options over full-sequence memory with 2 cross-attention layers, conditions
each field through 4 transformer decoder layers with a learned type embedding,
and scores options via a dual path: a lexical prior plus a joint interaction
MLP over $[\mathbf{f}_q; \mathbf{o}; \mathbf{f}_q\odot\mathbf{o};
|\mathbf{f}_q-\mathbf{o}|]$. Inference is one forward pass; outputs are
per-question softmax probabilities over \texttt{choice}, \texttt{score}, and
\texttt{noul} primitives. Context length is 4\,096 so full 77/150-option
schemas fit untruncated---Section~\ref{sec:results} shows why this matters.

\subsection{Multi-Objective Loss}
\begin{equation}
\mathcal{L} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{LS-CE}} + \lambda_{\text{Brier}}\,
\mathcal{L}_{\text{Brier}} + \lambda_{\text{ord}}\,\mathcal{L}_{\text{Ordinal}},
\end{equation}
combining label-smoothed cross-entropy ($\epsilon=0.05$), a Brier calibration
penalty against one-hot targets that punishes overconfident errors, and an
ordinal partial-credit term for \texttt{score} types.

\subsection{Post-Hoc Calibration}
Dual temperature scaling $z' = z/(T_{\text{head}}\cdot T_{\text{rel}}[\tau])$ is
fit with L-BFGS on the calibration split only, minimizing NLL over hard labels.
