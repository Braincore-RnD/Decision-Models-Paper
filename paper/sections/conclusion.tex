\section{Conclusion}
A 350M hybrid convolution-attention backbone, fitted with a joint schema head,
is a viable System-One decision engine: 81.3\%/72.9\% in-distribution means
over three seeds, 44\%/55.6\% held-out-taxonomy transfer, and honest,
reproduced numbers throughout. Our main methodological finding is a cautionary
one: a dramatic augmentation effect was context truncation in disguise, and
calibration fit in-distribution collapses under shift even as error detection
survives. Artifacts---seven training runs, six evaluation kernels, per-item
dumps, and logs---are archived for reproduction. Future work separates
augmentation types, extends OOS/OOD and leaderboard slices, and explores
shift-aware calibration.
